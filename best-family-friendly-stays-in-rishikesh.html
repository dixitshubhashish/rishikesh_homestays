Choosing family-friendly stays in Rishikesh

With children, look for a stay with a family room or a kitchen, easy road access and a lift if there are many stairs. Areas a short drive from the busiest ghats are calmer, and the river is fast, so keep little ones away from the water's edge.

Best Family-Friendly Stays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many family-friendly stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 58 family-friendly stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 12 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (27), Laxman Jhula (7), Swarg Ashram (3).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for family-friendly stays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Swarg Ashram is the ashram side across the river, near Parmarth Niketan, with mostly pedestrian lanes. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a family-friendly stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical family-friendly stay at about ₹4,800 a night, with most between ₹2,900 and ₹9,600. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly family-friendly stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 10 of the 58 family-friendly stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
