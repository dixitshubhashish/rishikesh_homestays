Quick facts: Meerut to Rishikesh by road

  • Meerut to Rishikesh is about 165 km by road.
  • Plan on about 3.5 to 4.5 hours on a clear run; traffic and the way out of Meerut decide the rest.
  • Take the old highway (NH-334) through Muzaffarnagar, Roorkee and Haridwar, then the 25 km run up the river to Rishikesh.
  • Leave Meerut before 7 am: the old highway through Muzaffarnagar and Roorkee is slow once the day's traffic is out.
  • Haridwar to Rishikesh is a 25 km drive up the river.

Getting out of Meerut

Meerut is on the old highway itself: from here it is straight up NH-334, still called NH-58 by everyone, through Muzaffarnagar and Roorkee to Haridwar.

Meerut to Rishikesh by road: questions travellers ask

How far is Rishikesh from Meerut by road?

It is about 165 km by road, roughly 3.5 to 4.5 hours depending on traffic and the route.

Which route should I take from Meerut to Rishikesh?

Take the old highway (NH-334) through Muzaffarnagar, Roorkee and Haridwar, then the 25 km run up the river to Rishikesh. Meerut is on the old highway itself: from here it is straight up NH-334, still called NH-58 by everyone, through Muzaffarnagar and Roorkee to Haridwar.

When is the best time to leave for Rishikesh?

Before 6 am, and never on a Friday evening if you can help it. Avoid the last week of the Kanwar Yatra in the month of Sawan, roughly July to August, and the main Kumbh 2027 bathing days.
