We ask guests about group size, budget, and preferences before sending them your way — fewer mismatched enquiries.

You stay in control

You set your own price, availability, and house rules. We pass on enquiries; you confirm bookings directly.

Marketing support

Your property gets featured across our homestays page, WhatsApp enquiries, and social channels at no extra cost.

Direct WhatsApp line

Guest enquiries and host coordination both happen over WhatsApp — no unfamiliar dashboard to learn.

Local, on-ground team

How it works

From application to your first guest, in four simple steps.

1

Apply

Share your property details using the form below — rooms, area, amenities, and a few photos if you have them.

3

Go live

4

Receive bookings

We forward matched guest enquiries directly to you by WhatsApp or phone. You confirm and host as usual.

What we look for

We keep a short, honest bar so travellers can trust every listing.

  • Clean, private or semi-private rooms with basic amenities (bed, fan/heater, attached or nearby washroom)
  • A real, reachable point of contact for guest coordination and check-in
  • Accurate description of your property — area, distance from the river/ghats, parking, kitchen access, Wi-Fi
  • Fair, transparent pricing with no hidden charges disclosed after booking
  • Willingness to confirm or decline guest enquiries within 24 hours
