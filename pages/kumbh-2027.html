Planning ahead

Haridwar's Kumbh Mela 2027: what's known, and why Rishikesh is a good base.

About 25 km from Rishikesh, Haridwar hosts one of Hinduism's great Kumbh Mela gatherings. Here's what's reliable to plan around today, and what isn't yet.

The Kumbh cycle, and where 2027 fits

Haridwar is one of four Kumbh Mela sites in India, hosting a full Purna Kumbh on a cycle of roughly twelve years, with a smaller Ardh Kumbh (half Kumbh) around the six-year midpoint. The last Haridwar Kumbh was held in 2021. By that pattern, 2027 falls as the next major gathering — and several reports suggest religious and state authorities intend to mark it with the scale of a full Kumbh rather than a smaller one. That said, this is a decision layered on top of the traditional cycle, not a change to the underlying astrological calculation, and it hasn't been treated as fully settled in the sources we've checked.

Reported dates for 2027 — not yet officially confirmed

Some tour operators are publishing a specific 2027 window: 14 January to 20 April 2027, with the following dates reported as Shahi Snan (royal bathing) days —

  • 14 January — Makar Sankranti
  • 6 February — Mauni Amavasya
  • 11 February — Vasant Panchami
  • 20 February — Magh Purnima
  • 6 March — Mahashivratri
  • 8 March — Somvati Amavasya
  • 7 April — Nav Samvatsar
  • 14 April — Mesh Sankranti / Baisakhi
  • 15 April — Ram Navami
  • 20 April — Chaitra Purnima

Treat this list as reported, not confirmed. The tour sites publishing it attribute the dates to a meeting between Uttarakhand's Chief Minister and the Akhil Bharatiya Akhara Parishad, but none link to an official government or Kumbh Mela administration notification. Wikipedia's own record of the Haridwar Kumbh cycle still lists 2033 as the next event under the strict twelve-year Purna Kumbh count — a real discrepancy that isn't resolved in public sources as of this writing. Exact dates are ultimately set by astrological calculation (the Panchang) and confirmed officially closer to the event. If you are planning travel around the Kumbh, check Uttarakhand's official tourism channels before booking, and treat every date above as provisional until then.

Why Rishikesh makes sense as a base

A Kumbh Mela draws enormous crowds — the 2021 Haridwar Kumbh, even in a curtailed form, drew millions of visitors. A full-scale 2027 event could draw substantially more. Rishikesh sits a short drive away and offers a calmer alternative to competing for lodging directly in Haridwar during the mela — many travellers already use it as a base for visiting Haridwar's regular Ganga Aarti and festivals, and the same logic applies at Kumbh scale. Staying in Rishikesh also means you're already positioned for the rest of a Garhwal trip — yoga, rafting, and the routes north toward Kedarnath and the Char Dham — before or after visiting the Kumbh.

How Rishikesh and Haridwar compare

The two towns share the Ganga but feel different. Haridwar is older and more ritual-dense — its Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri, run by hereditary priest families at the Brahma Kund, is louder and more traditional than Rishikesh's own aarti at Parmarth Niketan. Rishikesh blends that same river spirituality with yoga, wellness, and adventure tourism. Read more in our About Rishikesh guide.
