Trip planning

Things to do in Rishikesh without rushing the city.

Choose where you want to visit first, then stay in the area that makes those experiences easy to reach.

Best first-time experiences

Rishikesh works best when your days have space. Pick one anchor experience each day, stay close to the places you want to explore, and leave room for cafes, river walks, and quiet time at your homestay.

Make a half-day temple trip

Visit Neelkanth Mahadev Temple outside town, then keep the rest of the day open for a slow meal or riverside time. Arrange transport before you leave.

Best season: October to March
Best time: Morning
Context: A revered Shiva temple and a peaceful Garhwal foothills drive.

Stay near Neelkanth Road

Plan a Kunjapuri sunrise

Leave before dawn for the hilltop temple and sunrise viewpoint, then return for breakfast and an easier afternoon in Rishikesh.

Best season: October to April
Best time: Before sunrise
Context: A hilltop Devi temple with wide views across the Himalayan foothills.

Stay near your sunrise route

Walk to Neer Garh Waterfall

Pair a short waterfall walk with a Tapovan morning. Check current water and trail conditions, especially after heavy rain.

Best season: Post-monsoon to spring
Best time: Morning or early afternoon
Context: A forested nature stop reached by a short walk from the Tapovan side.

Stay near Tapovan

Spend a morning on the river

River rafting is a popular adventure choice. Use a licensed operator, ask about current river conditions, and confirm that the route suits your group.

Best season: September to June, subject to local approval
Best time: Morning departures
Context: Rishikesh is one of Uttarakhand’s best-known white-water adventure hubs.

Stay near river activities

End the day at Ganga Aarti

Plan an evening at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan, arrive early, and leave time for a quiet walk after the ceremony.

Best season: Year-round, with cooler evenings October to March
Best time: Dusk; confirm the day’s start time locally
Context: A daily river ritual of lamps, music, prayer, and community gathering.

Stay near Triveni Ghat

Make room for yoga and quiet

Choose a class, ashram visit, or unhurried riverside walk. Tapovan, Swarg Ashram, and Muni Ki Reti each suit a different pace.

Best season: October to March for comfortable mornings
Best time: Early morning
Context: Rishikesh’s ashrams and yoga traditions make slow, intentional mornings part of the destination.

Stay near the ashrams

One-day easy itinerary

  1. Start with sunrise tea and a riverside walk.
  2. Take a morning yoga class or visit an ashram area.
  3. Have lunch in Tapovan or near Muni Ki Reti.
  4. Visit a waterfall, bridge area, or market depending on energy.
  5. End with Ganga Aarti and a simple dinner near your stay.

Good questions before booking activities

  • Is pickup included from your homestay area?
  • What is the current safety guidance for rafting or waterfall walks?
  • Does the activity suit children, seniors, or non-swimmers?
  • What should you carry, and what should you avoid carrying?

Plan around the season

October to March is usually the easiest window for comfortable sightseeing, yoga mornings, temple visits, and clear hill views. Rafting generally runs from September to June, but river conditions, weather, and local permissions can change. During the monsoon, keep plans flexible and confirm waterfall, road, and activity access before setting out.

Choose your Rishikesh season

Spring: February to March

Comfortable days for walking, sunrise trips, yoga, cafes, and riverfront exploring. Festival energy can make the town especially lively, so reserve your stay early.

Stay near yoga and ashrams

Summer: April to June

Plan outdoor time for early morning and evening, then use the middle of the day for cafes, rest, or a homestay with a shaded terrace. Confirm rafting availability before booking.

Stay near cafes and walks

Monsoon: July to September

Expect lush hills, heavier rain, slippery trails, and changing river levels. Rafting is generally suspended during the monsoon; check waterfall and road access locally.

Find a flexible stay base

Autumn and winter: October to January

Good for sightseeing, temples, bridges, aarti, photography, and sunny riverside walks. Pack a light warm layer for early mornings and evenings.

Stay near the riverfront

Slow days between bigger plans

Not every day needs a booked activity. Walk a ghat at sunrise or sunset, cross a suspension bridge, browse the market, sit beside a river beach only where access is safe, or spend an afternoon at a riverside cafe. Rishikesh is also a practical overnight base before heading toward Devprayag, Kedarnath, Badrinath, or other Garhwal routes.
