Best Cottages in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many cottages are there in Rishikesh?

There are 45 cottages in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 13 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (22), Muni Ki Reti (4), Triveni Ghat (3).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for cottages?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Muni Ki Reti is between Ram Jhula and the town, with ashrams, ghats and easy road access; Triveni Ghat is the town centre, with the big evening aarti, markets and the bus stand nearby. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a cottage in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical cottage at about ₹3,000 a night, with most between ₹1,900 and ₹4,200. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly cottages in Rishikesh?

Yes. 16 of the 45 cottages here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
