Sacred riverfront

Triveni Ghat: evening aarti, river steps, and local Rishikesh life.

One of the most important riverfront places in Rishikesh, especially for travellers who want to experience the Ganga Aarti respectfully.

Why visit Triveni Ghat?

Triveni Ghat is a central Rishikesh riverfront known for ritual bathing, evening Ganga Aarti, nearby temples, and local markets. It is a meaningful stop for pilgrims and a memorable evening experience for first-time visitors.

Ganga Aarti at dusk

The evening ceremony is the main draw. The exact start changes with sunset and local arrangements, so ask your host on the day.

River views and steps

Come earlier for a calmer walk, river views, bathing ghats, and time to find a respectful place before the evening crowd.

Temples and local market

Keep time for the nearby temple and market streets. Carry small notes, water, and only what you need for the riverfront.

What you will find there?

Expect broad river steps, bathing areas, temple access, flower and prayer-item sellers, nearby food and market streets, auto-rickshaws, and places to pause by the Ganga. Facilities and crowd levels vary, so keep valuables secure and use public areas respectfully.

How to plan the visit?

  • Arrive before sunset to find a comfortable place without rushing.
  • Dress modestly and keep the steps clear for worshippers.
  • Carry only essentials; evenings can become busy.
  • Avoid putting plastic, flowers, or packaging into the river.
  • Check with your host for the current aarti timing before leaving.

Reach Triveni Ghat from your homestay

Many of our stays are around 6–7 km from Triveni Ghat. By auto-rickshaw, car, or taxi, allow roughly 15–25 minutes in normal city traffic; a bike can be quicker when roads are clear. A 5–10 minute ride is possible only from a much closer starting point or in unusually light traffic, so leave early for aarti and confirm the pickup spot with your host.

Auto-rickshaw

Easy for short groups and flexible pickup. Agree the fare or use a known local driver before leaving.

Car or taxi

More comfortable for families, older guests, or evening return trips. Ask for a drop point near the ghat steps.

Bike or scooter

Useful when you want to combine Triveni Ghat with Bharat Mandir, markets, or another riverside stop. Confirm parking locally.

Where to stay nearby?

You do not need to stay beside the ghat to enjoy it. Choose one of our quieter stays 6–7 km away, travel in for the evening, and return to a more relaxed base after the crowds thin. Choose Tapovan or Swarg Ashram if cafes, yoga, and quieter walking routes matter more.
