Best Stays with a Jacuzzi in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many stays with a jacuzzi are there in Haridwar?

There are 9 stays with a jacuzzi in Haridwar on this page, spread across 5 areas. The biggest clusters are Kankhal (3), Railway Station (1), Rishikesh Road & Motichur (1).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for stays with a jacuzzi?

It depends on the trip. Kankhal is an older, quieter neighbourhood of ashrams and temples south of the centre; Railway Station is handy for trains, a short ride from the ghats; Rishikesh Road & Motichur is on the road towards Rishikesh, near Motichur and Chandi Devi. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a stay with a jacuzzi in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay with a jacuzzi at about ₹4,200 a night, with most between ₹2,600 and ₹12,800. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays with a jacuzzi in Haridwar?

Yes. 2 of the 9 stays with a jacuzzi here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
