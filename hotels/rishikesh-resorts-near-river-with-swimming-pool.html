Rishikesh Resorts near River with Swimming Pool: tips before you book

Staying by the Ganga puts the ghats and the evening Ganga aarti at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan within reach on foot. The riverside stretches are Tapovan, Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula, Swarg Ashram and Muni Ki Reti. A room that actually faces the river usually costs more than one at the back, so ask which side yours is on.

Pools in the hills are often seasonal; ask whether the pool is open, and heated, when you travel.

Resorts mostly sit outside the walkable centre, on the Neelkanth and Badrinath roads or by the river upstream, so budget for taxis or a car. Ask whether meals are included and how far it is to the ghats for the evening aarti.

Rishikesh Resorts near River with Swimming Pool: questions travellers ask

How many resorts near River with Swimming Pool are there in Rishikesh?

There are 24 resorts near River with Swimming Pool in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 5 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (13), Shivpuri & rafting belt (4), Laxman Jhula (2).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for resorts near River with Swimming Pool?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Shivpuri & rafting belt is upstream on the Badrinath road where most rafting starts, with riverside camps; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river.

How much does a resort in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical resort at about ₹5,700 a night, with most between ₹4,800 and ₹11,900. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
