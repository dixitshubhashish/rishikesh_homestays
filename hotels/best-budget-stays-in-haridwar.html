Choosing budget stays in Haridwar

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

  • Most budget stays are in Bhupatwala (30), Railway Station (29), Rishikesh Road & Motichur (16).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹1,200 a night; the middle half of budget stays start between ₹1,100 and ₹1,400 (based on 151 listings with a price).
  • 122 have a star rating, including 2 five-star and 18 four-star.
  • 28 (19%) allow pets and 29 (19%) are on or facing the Ganga.
  • Of the 76 with at least five guest reviews, 14 score 9/10 or higher.

Coming for the Kumbh 2027?

Haridwar fills up months ahead of the Kumbh. Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at, expect road closures around the big snan days, and book early. For a calmer base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver.

Best Budget Stays in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many budget stays are there in Haridwar?

There are 151 budget stays in Haridwar on this page, spread across 13 areas. The biggest clusters are Bhupatwala (30), Railway Station (29), Rishikesh Road & Motichur (16).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for budget stays?

It depends on the trip. Bhupatwala is an ashram-lined stretch on the way towards Rishikesh, calmer than the centre; Railway Station is handy for trains, a short ride from the ghats; Rishikesh Road & Motichur is on the road towards Rishikesh, near Motichur and Chandi Devi. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a budget stay in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical budget stay at about ₹1,200 a night, with most between ₹1,100 and ₹1,400. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly budget stays in Haridwar?
