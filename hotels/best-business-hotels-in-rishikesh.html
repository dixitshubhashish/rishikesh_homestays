Best Business Hotels in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many business hotels are there in Rishikesh?

There are 124 business hotels in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 17 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (55), Laxman Jhula (10), Neelkanth Road (8).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for business hotels?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Neelkanth Road is the hilly road towards Neelkanth Mahadev, with resorts and camps; you will need a vehicle.

How much does a business hotel in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical business hotel at about ₹4,500 a night, with most between ₹2,700 and ₹8,200. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly business hotels in Rishikesh?

Yes. 34 of the 124 business hotels here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest business hotel on this page?

Skyard Premium, Tapovan in Tapovan has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹191 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.

Which business hotels here have the best guest reviews?

The best-reviewed are Kinwani House – Araiya Anthology Hotel (9.6/10), Taj Rishikesh Resort & Spa Uttarakhand (9.5/10), Rishikesh Valley (9.2/10), counting only stays with at least ten reviews.
