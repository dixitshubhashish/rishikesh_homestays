Best Long Stays with a Kitchen in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many long stays with a kitchen are there in Rishikesh?

There are 264 long stays with a kitchen in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 17 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (120), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (22), Laxman Jhula (21).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for long stays with a kitchen?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a long stay with a kitchen in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical long stay with a kitchen at about ₹3,000 a night, with most between ₹1,800 and ₹6,100. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly long stays with a kitchen in Rishikesh?

Yes. 105 of the 264 long stays with a kitchen here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
