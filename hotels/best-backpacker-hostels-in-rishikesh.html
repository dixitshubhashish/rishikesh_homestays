Choosing backpacker hostels in Rishikesh

Backpacker hostels are where solo travellers find rafting buddies, trek partners and the best cafe tips. Most sit in Tapovan, walkable to Laxman Jhula and the yoga schools. Compare mixed and female-only dorms, check for lockers and hot water, and book ahead for long weekends and the New Year rush.

  • Most backpacker hostels are in Tapovan (101), Laxman Jhula (33), Neelkanth Road (4).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹1,900 a night; the middle half of backpacker hostels start between ₹1,300 and ₹3,300 (based on 116 listings with a price).
  • 9 have a star rating, including 0 five-star and 2 four-star.
  • 59 (40%) allow pets and 10 (7%) are on or facing the Ganga.
  • Of the 84 with at least five guest reviews, 6 score 9/10 or higher.

Best Backpacker Hostels in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many backpacker hostels are there in Rishikesh?

There are 147 backpacker hostels in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 9 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (101), Laxman Jhula (33), Neelkanth Road (4).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for backpacker hostels?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Neelkanth Road is the hilly road towards Neelkanth Mahadev, with resorts and camps; you will need a vehicle. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a backpacker hostel in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical backpacker hostel at about ₹1,900 a night, with most between ₹1,300 and ₹3,300. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly backpacker hostels in Rishikesh?

Yes. 59 of the 147 backpacker hostels here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
