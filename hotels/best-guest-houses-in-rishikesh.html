Best Guest Houses in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many guest houses are there in Rishikesh?

There are 116 guest houses in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 14 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (38), Laxman Jhula (19), Swarg Ashram (10).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for guest houses?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Swarg Ashram is the ashram side across the river, near Parmarth Niketan, with mostly pedestrian lanes.

How much does a guest house in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical guest house at about ₹2,100 a night, with most between ₹1,300 and ₹2,900. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly guest houses in Rishikesh?

Yes. 20 of the 116 guest houses here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest guest house on this page?

Krishna Paying Guest House in Laxman Jhula has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹383 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.

Which guest houses here have the best guest reviews?
