Choosing studio & 1 BHK stays in Rishikesh

A studio or 1 BHK gives you your own front door and somewhere to cook for less than most hotel suites, which is why yoga students and remote workers settle into them for weeks. Most are in apartment blocks, so ask which floor it is on and whether there is a lift, whether the kitchen has a proper stove or just a kettle, how long the power backup lasts, and whether there is parking if you are driving up. Weekly and monthly rates are usually far below the nightly price, so always ask.

Best Studio & 1 BHK Stays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many studio & 1 BHK stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 46 studio & 1 BHK stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 5 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (36), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (5), Veerbhadra Temple (3).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for studio & 1 BHK stays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a studio or 1 BHK stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical studio or 1 BHK stay at about ₹3,400 a night, with most between ₹2,000 and ₹4,500. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly studio & 1 BHK stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 27 of the 46 studio & 1 BHK stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest studio or 1 BHK stay on this page?

Homlee – Jumbo 1BHK-2Ac – Kitchen – Lift – Parking – Tapovan in Tapovan has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹1,436 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.

Which studio & 1 BHK stays here have the best guest reviews?
