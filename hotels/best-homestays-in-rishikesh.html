Choosing homestays in Rishikesh

A homestay suits travellers who want home food, local advice and a calmer lane rather than a front desk. Most are small, so ask about hot water, parking and stairs before you book, and agree on meal timings with the family.

Best Homestays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many homestays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 129 homestays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 13 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (51), Veerbhadra Temple (14), Muni Ki Reti (11).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for homestays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Muni Ki Reti is between Ram Jhula and the town, with ashrams, ghats and easy road access. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a homestay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical homestay at about ₹2,100 a night, with most between ₹1,100 and ₹3,000. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly homestays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 36 of the 129 homestays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
