Best Homestays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many homestays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 122 homestays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 13 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (47), Veerbhadra Temple (14), Muni Ki Reti (12).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for homestays?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Muni Ki Reti is between Ram Jhula and the town, with ashrams, ghats and easy road access.
