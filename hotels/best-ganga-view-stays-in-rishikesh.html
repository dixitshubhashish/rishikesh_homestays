Best Ganga View Stays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many ganga view stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 246 ganga view stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 15 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (77), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (30), Laxman Jhula (23).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for ganga view stays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a Ganga view stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical Ganga view stay at about ₹5,000 a night, with most between ₹3,000 and ₹9,600. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly ganga view stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 83 of the 246 ganga view stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
