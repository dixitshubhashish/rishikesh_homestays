Staying near Triveni Ghat: good to know

The ghat is busiest just before the evening aarti; stays within a kilometre let you walk there and back after dark.

Visiting on a festival day? Expect closed lanes and crowds around the ghats and temples, plan to walk the last stretch, and book ahead for weekends.
