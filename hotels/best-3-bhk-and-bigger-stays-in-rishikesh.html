Choosing 3 BHK and bigger stays in Rishikesh

For a big family or a group of friends, one 3 BHK or larger home usually beats juggling several hotel rooms: everyone stays under one roof, shares a kitchen and living room, and the cost per person drops. Our own Advaitam is exactly that, a luxury 3 BHK by the Ganges ghat in quiet Nirmal Bagh, close to the river without the crowds; message us for direct rates. Wherever you book, ask how many bathrooms there are, whether the bedrooms are on one floor or up several flights of stairs (and if there is a lift), whether there is parking for more than one car, whether a caretaker or cook is on hand, and what the power backup covers.

Best 3 BHK and Bigger Stays in Rishikesh for Families & Groups: questions travellers ask

How many stays with 3 or more bedrooms are there in Rishikesh?

There are 18 stays with 3 or more bedrooms in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 10 areas. The biggest clusters are Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (3), Veerbhadra Temple (2), Tapovan (2).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for stays with 3 or more bedrooms?

It depends on the trip. Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a 3 BHK or bigger stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical 3 BHK or bigger stay at about ₹27,400 a night, with most between ₹12,000 and ₹46,300. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays with 3 or more bedrooms in Rishikesh?

Yes. 6 of the 18 stays with 3 or more bedrooms here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
