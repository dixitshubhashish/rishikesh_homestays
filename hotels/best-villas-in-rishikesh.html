Best Villas in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many villas are there in Rishikesh?

There are 47 villas in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 14 areas. The biggest clusters are Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (10), Tapovan (6), Haridwar Road (4).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for villas?

It depends on the trip. Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Haridwar Road is on the road towards Haridwar, handy for road trips. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a villa in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical villa at about ₹11,600 a night, with most between ₹3,400 and ₹33,800. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly villas in Rishikesh?

Yes. 20 of the 47 villas here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
