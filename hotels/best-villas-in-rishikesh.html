Best Villas in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many villas are there in Rishikesh?

There are 63 villas in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 16 areas. The biggest clusters are Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (10), Tapovan (7), Raiwala & Shyampur (5).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for villas?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Raiwala & Shyampur is on the Haridwar side of Rishikesh, convenient for Haridwar and the highway.

How much does a villa in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical villa at about ₹11,600 a night, with most between ₹3,400 and ₹33,800. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly villas in Rishikesh?

Yes. 20 of the 63 villas here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest villa on this page?

Nature Villa in Elsewhere in Rishikesh has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹1,053 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.

Which villas here have the best guest reviews?

The best-reviewed are Atreya Boutique Cottage near Tapovan (9.2/10), Yog Niketan Villa (9.1/10), Ganga Euphoria – A Luxury Villa on the Ganges (8.5/10), counting only stays with at least ten reviews.
