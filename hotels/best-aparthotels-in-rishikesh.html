Best Aparthotels in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many aparthotels are there in Rishikesh?

There are 16 aparthotels in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 5 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (12), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (1), Haridwar Road (1).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for aparthotels?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Haridwar Road is on the road towards Haridwar, handy for road trips. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a aparthotel in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical aparthotel at about ₹4,100 a night, with most between ₹1,800 and ₹4,700. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly aparthotels in Rishikesh?

Yes. 5 of the 16 aparthotels here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
