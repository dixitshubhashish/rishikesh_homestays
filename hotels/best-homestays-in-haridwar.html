Choosing homestays in Haridwar

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

  • Most homestays are in Kankhal (10), Ranipur & BHEL (5), Bhupatwala (2).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹2,900 a night; the middle half of homestays start between ₹1,700 and ₹5,100 (based on 24 listings with a price).

Haridwar fills up months ahead of the Kumbh. Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at, expect road closures around the big snan days, and book early. For a calmer base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver.

Best Homestays in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many homestays are there in Haridwar?

There are 32 homestays in Haridwar on this page, spread across 12 areas. The biggest clusters are Kankhal (10), Ranipur & BHEL (5), Bhupatwala (2).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for homestays?

It depends on the trip. Kankhal is an older, quieter neighbourhood of ashrams and temples south of the centre; Ranipur & BHEL is the BHEL township side, quieter and good for driving in; Bhupatwala is an ashram-lined stretch on the way towards Rishikesh, calmer than the centre. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a homestay in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical homestay at about ₹2,900 a night, with most between ₹1,700 and ₹5,100. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly homestays in Haridwar?

Yes. 13 of the 32 homestays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
