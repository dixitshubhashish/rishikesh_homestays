3 Star Resorts in Mussoorie: what to know

Mussoorie is a long ridge town, so where you sleep decides how you spend the day. The Mall Road between Library Chowk and Kulri is the lively centre, with shops, cafes and the evening walk; Library Chowk and Charleville, at the western end, are older and a little calmer; Camel's Back Road and Gun Hill are for slow evening strolls; Landour and Char Dukan, to the east, are quieter, with old cottages and bakeries; Barlowganj and Jharipani sit on the slopes below; and the Kempty Road runs out towards Kempty Falls, with many of the resorts and views. On busy weekends and holidays the Mall Road area gets crowded, so ask your stay how far a car can go and how much of the way is on foot.

3 Star Resorts in Mussoorie: questions travellers ask

How many 3 star resorts are there in Mussoorie?

There are 32 3 star resorts in Mussoorie on this page, spread across 8 areas. The biggest clusters are Kempty Road (14), Mussoorie Lake & Dehradun Road (5), Library Chowk & Charleville (2).

Which area of Mussoorie is best for 3 star resorts?

For the Mall Road, cafes and evening walks, stay around Library Chowk or Kulri; for quiet, try Landour or Camel's Back Road; for views and the falls, the Kempty Road. Kempty Road is the road out towards Kempty Falls, with resorts and views; you will want a vehicle; Mussoorie Lake & Dehradun Road is the Dehradun side of town, around Mussoorie Lake, the first stretch on the way up from Dehradun; Library Chowk & Charleville is the western end of the Mall Road, a little calmer than Kulri, with the old library and shops.

How much does a resort in Mussoorie cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical resort at about ₹5,000 a night, with most between ₹2,700 and ₹8,600. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly 3 star resorts in Mussoorie?

Yes. 2 of the 32 3 star resorts here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
