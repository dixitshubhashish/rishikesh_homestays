Where to stay in Rishikesh

Rishikesh splits into a few very different bases. Tapovan and Laxman Jhula are the walkable cafe-and-yoga side; Swarg Ashram and Muni Ki Reti sit by the ashrams and ghats; Triveni Ghat is the busy town centre; the Neelkanth and Badrinath roads hold the resorts and riverside camps that need a car. How many stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 1,700 stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 19 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (819), Laxman Jhula (169), Veerbhadra Temple (79).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for stays?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats.

Listed starting prices put a typical stay at about ₹0 a night, with most between ₹0 and ₹100. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
