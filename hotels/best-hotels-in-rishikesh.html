Best Hotels in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 2,496 stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 19 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (963), Laxman Jhula (169), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (104).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for stays?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats.

How much does a stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay at about ₹3,100 a night, with most between ₹1,900 and ₹5,300. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 444 of the 2,496 stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
