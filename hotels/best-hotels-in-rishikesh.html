Where to stay in Rishikesh

Rishikesh splits into a few very different bases. Tapovan and Laxman Jhula are the walkable cafe-and-yoga side; Swarg Ashram and Muni Ki Reti sit by the ashrams and ghats; Triveni Ghat is the busy town centre; the Neelkanth and Badrinath roads hold the resorts and riverside camps that need a car. Pick the base first, then the type of stay.

  • Most stays are in Tapovan (833), Laxman Jhula (151), Veerbhadra Temple (67).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹3,100 a night; the middle half of stays start between ₹1,900 and ₹5,300 (based on 1113 listings with a price).
  • 733 have a star rating, including 48 five-star and 154 four-star.
  • 444 (25%) allow pets and 259 (15%) are on or facing the Ganga.
  • Of the 552 with at least five guest reviews, 81 score 9/10 or higher.

Best Hotels in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 1,742 stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 19 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (833), Laxman Jhula (151), Veerbhadra Temple (67).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for stays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay at about ₹3,100 a night, with most between ₹1,900 and ₹5,300. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 444 of the 1,742 stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
