Cheap Apartments in Rishikesh: tips before you book

Prices are each stay's listed starting price per night, usually for its smallest room. Weekends, long weekends and the cooler months from October to March fill up first, and prices rise then; quieter months cost less. Staying a week or more? Ask for a weekly rate, many small places will agree.

Cheaper rooms are often simpler, so check the basics before you book: hot water, a fan or AC, Wi-Fi if you need it, and whether the room is up several flights of stairs.

Which area of Rishikesh is best for cheap apartments?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats.
