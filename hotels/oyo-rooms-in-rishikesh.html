OYO Rooms in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many OYO rooms are there in Rishikesh?

There are 88 OYO rooms in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 15 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (34), Triveni Ghat (6), AIIMS Rishikesh (5).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for OYO rooms?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Triveni Ghat is the town centre, with the big evening aarti, markets and the bus stand nearby; AIIMS Rishikesh is near the AIIMS hospital on the Haridwar side, practical for hospital visits.

How much does a stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay at about ₹2,300 a night, with most between ₹1,500 and ₹3,200. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly OYO rooms in Rishikesh?

Yes. 4 of the 88 OYO rooms here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest stay on this page?
