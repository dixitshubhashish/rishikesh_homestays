Top 10 Homestays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many top 10 homestays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 10 top 10 homestays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 4 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (6), Veerbhadra Temple (2), Triveni Ghat (1).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for top 10 homestays?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Triveni Ghat is the town centre, with the big evening aarti, markets and the bus stand nearby.

How much does a homestay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical homestay at about ₹1,100 a night, with most between ₹1,000 and ₹1,800. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly top 10 homestays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 2 of the 10 top 10 homestays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest homestay on this page?

Kamlkash Niwas Homestay in Tapovan has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹670 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.
