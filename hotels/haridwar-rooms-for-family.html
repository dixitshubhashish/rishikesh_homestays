Haridwar Rooms for Family: what to know

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

  • Most rooms for family are in Har Ki Pauri (18), Railway Station (17), Kankhal (12).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹3,400 a night; the middle half of rooms for family start between ₹2,500 and ₹5,900 (based on 100 listings with a price).
  • 83 have a star rating, including 13 five-star and 27 four-star.
  • 22 (17%) allow pets and 33 (26%) are on or facing the Ganga.
  • Of the 71 with at least five guest reviews, 15 score 9/10 or higher.

Haridwar Rooms for Family: questions travellers ask

How many rooms for family are there in Haridwar?

There are 128 rooms for family in Haridwar on this page, spread across 14 areas. The biggest clusters are Har Ki Pauri (18), Railway Station (17), Kankhal (12).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for rooms for family?

It depends on the trip. Har Ki Pauri is the main ghat and pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti; Railway Station is handy for trains, a short ride from the ghats; Kankhal is an older, quieter neighbourhood of ashrams and temples south of the centre. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a stay in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay at about ₹3,400 a night, with most between ₹2,500 and ₹5,900. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
