Where to stay in Mussoorie

Mussoorie is a long ridge town, so where you sleep decides how you spend the day. The Mall Road between Library Chowk and Kulri is the lively centre, with shops, cafes and the evening walk; Library Chowk and Charleville, at the western end, are older and a little calmer; Camel's Back Road and Gun Hill are for slow evening strolls; Landour and Char Dukan, to the east, are quieter, with old cottages and bakeries; Barlowganj and Jharipani sit on the slopes below; and the Kempty Road runs out towards Kempty Falls, with many of the resorts and views. On busy weekends and holidays the Mall Road area gets crowded, so ask your stay how far a car can go and how much of the way is on foot.

Best Hotels in Mussoorie: questions travellers ask

How many stays are there in Mussoorie?

There are 1,218 stays in Mussoorie on this page, spread across 11 areas. The biggest clusters are Mall Road & Kulri (305), Kempty Road (239), Library Chowk & Charleville (191).

Which area of Mussoorie is best for stays?

For the Mall Road, cafes and evening walks, stay around Library Chowk or Kulri; for quiet, try Landour or Camel's Back Road; for views and the falls, the Kempty Road. Mall Road & Kulri is the lively centre, with the Mall Road, Kulri bazaar, cafes and the evening walk; Kempty Road is the road out towards Kempty Falls, with resorts and views; you will want a vehicle; Library Chowk & Charleville is the western end of the Mall Road, a little calmer than Kulri, with the old library and shops.

How much does a stay in Mussoorie cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay at about ₹4,200 a night, with most between ₹2,600 and ₹7,500. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays in Mussoorie?

Yes. 255 of the 1,218 stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
