Best Stays with a Jacuzzi in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many stays with a jacuzzi are there in Rishikesh?

There are 25 stays with a jacuzzi in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 11 areas. The biggest clusters are Veerbhadra Temple (6), Haridwar Road (3), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (2).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for stays with a jacuzzi?

It depends on the trip. Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Haridwar Road is on the road towards Haridwar, handy for road trips; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a stay with a jacuzzi in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay with a jacuzzi at about ₹14,300 a night, with most between ₹4,800 and ₹22,400. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays with a jacuzzi in Rishikesh?

Yes. 11 of the 25 stays with a jacuzzi here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
