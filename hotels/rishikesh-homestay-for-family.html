Rishikesh Homestay for Family: questions travellers ask

How many homestay for family are there in Rishikesh?

There are 136 homestay for family in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 17 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (47), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (16), Veerbhadra Temple (13).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for homestay for family?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats.

How much does a homestay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical homestay at about ₹100 a night, with most between ₹0 and ₹200. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly homestay for family in Rishikesh?

Yes. 46 of the 136 homestay for family here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
