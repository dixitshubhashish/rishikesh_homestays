Choosing family-friendly stays in Haridwar

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

  • Most family-friendly stays are in Har Ki Pauri (15), Railway Station (14), Upper Road & Mayapur (7).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹3,900 a night; the middle half of family-friendly stays start between ₹2,500 and ₹6,000 (based on 57 listings with a price).
  • 47 have a star rating, including 10 five-star and 16 four-star.
  • 8 (14%) allow pets and 19 (32%) are on or facing the Ganga.
  • Of the 52 with at least five guest reviews, 12 score 9/10 or higher.

Coming for the Kumbh 2027?

Haridwar fills up months ahead of the Kumbh. Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at, expect road closures around the big snan days, and book early. For a calmer base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver.

Best Family-Friendly Stays in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many family-friendly stays are there in Haridwar?

There are 59 family-friendly stays in Haridwar on this page, spread across 11 areas. The biggest clusters are Har Ki Pauri (15), Railway Station (14), Upper Road & Mayapur (7).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for family-friendly stays?

It depends on the trip. Har Ki Pauri is the main ghat and pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti; Railway Station is handy for trains, a short ride from the ghats; Upper Road & Mayapur is the bazaar strip behind the ghats, walkable to Har Ki Pauri. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a family-friendly stay in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical family-friendly stay at about ₹3,900 a night, with most between ₹2,500 and ₹6,000. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly family-friendly stays in Haridwar?

Yes. 8 of the 59 family-friendly stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
