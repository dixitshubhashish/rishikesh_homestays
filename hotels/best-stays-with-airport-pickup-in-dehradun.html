Choosing stays with airport pickup in Dehradun

Dehradun spreads out along a few main roads. The Clock Tower and Paltan Bazaar are the busy old centre; the railway station and the ISBT bus terminal sit to the south-west; the Rajpur Road runs north-east towards Rajpur and the road up to Mussoorie, with many of the cafes, restaurants and boutique stays. Sahastradhara Road and the Mussoorie Road foothills are greener and quieter, with villas, resorts and homestays; the Chakrata Road and the Haridwar Road are practical bases for the highway, and Jolly Grant airport is on the Rishikesh side. For a short trip, stay near the Rajpur Road or the Clock Tower; for a quiet one, go towards Sahastradhara or Malsi.

There are 145 stays with airport pickup in Dehradun on this page, spread across 17 areas. The biggest clusters are Mussoorie Road & Malsi (15), Rajpur Road (14), Saharanpur Road & Patel Nagar (13).

Which area of Dehradun is best for stays with airport pickup?

For cafes, restaurants and an evening walk, stay on the Rajpur Road or around the Clock Tower; for trains and buses, near the railway station or the ISBT; for quiet nights and green views, towards Sahastradhara Road, Malsi or the Mussoorie Road foothills. Mussoorie Road & Malsi is the foothills on the way up to Mussoorie, around Malsi, with villas and hillside homestays; Rajpur Road is the long, cafe-lined road from the Clock Tower towards Rajpur, popular for restaurants, shops and boutique stays; Saharanpur Road & Patel Nagar is the western side towards the Saharanpur Road, practical with easy road links towards Delhi.

How much does a stay with airport pickup in Dehradun cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay with airport pickup at about ₹3,200 a night, with most between ₹2,100 and ₹5,300. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays with airport pickup in Dehradun?

Yes. 56 of the 145 stays with airport pickup here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest stay with airport pickup on this page?

Property 1 in Railway Station & Tyagi Road has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹866 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.

Which stays with airport pickup here have the best guest reviews?

The best-reviewed are Nivasa by Nautiyals Apartment (9.4/10), Oxygen Homestay – A Blissful Retreat (9.3/10), Athulyam Homes (9.3/10), counting only stays with at least ten reviews.
