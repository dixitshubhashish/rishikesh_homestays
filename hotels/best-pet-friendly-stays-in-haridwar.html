Choosing pet-friendly stays in Haridwar

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

  • Most pet-friendly stays are in Bhupatwala (23), Rishikesh Road & Motichur (18), Har Ki Pauri (16).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹2,400 a night; the middle half of pet-friendly stays start between ₹1,500 and ₹3,500 (based on 121 listings with a price).
  • 109 have a star rating, including 12 five-star and 36 four-star.
  • 148 (100%) allow pets and 25 (17%) are on or facing the Ganga.
  • Of the 81 with at least five guest reviews, 16 score 9/10 or higher.

Coming for the Kumbh 2027?

Haridwar fills up months ahead of the Kumbh. Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at, expect road closures around the big snan days, and book early. For a calmer base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver.

Best Pet-Friendly Stays in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many pet-friendly stays are there in Haridwar?

There are 148 pet-friendly stays in Haridwar on this page, spread across 14 areas. The biggest clusters are Bhupatwala (23), Rishikesh Road & Motichur (18), Har Ki Pauri (16).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for pet-friendly stays?

It depends on the trip. Bhupatwala is an ashram-lined stretch on the way towards Rishikesh, calmer than the centre; Rishikesh Road & Motichur is on the road towards Rishikesh, near Motichur and Chandi Devi; Har Ki Pauri is the main ghat and pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a pet-friendly stay in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical pet-friendly stay at about ₹2,400 a night, with most between ₹1,500 and ₹3,500. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
