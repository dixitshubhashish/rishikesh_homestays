Best Lodges in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many lodges are there in Rishikesh?

There are 7 lodges in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 4 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (4), Muni Ki Reti (1), Swarg Ashram (1).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for lodges?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Muni Ki Reti is between Ram Jhula and the town, with ashrams, ghats and easy road access; Swarg Ashram is the ashram side across the river, near Parmarth Niketan, with mostly pedestrian lanes. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a lodge in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical lodge at about ₹4,200 a night, with most between ₹3,700 and ₹9,500. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly lodges in Rishikesh?

Yes. 2 of the 7 lodges here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
