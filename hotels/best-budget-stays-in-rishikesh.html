How many budget stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 187 budget stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 15 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (114), Laxman Jhula (28), Swarg Ashram (9).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for budget stays?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Swarg Ashram is the ashram side across the river, near Parmarth Niketan, with mostly pedestrian lanes.

How much does a budget stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical budget stay at about ₹1,200 a night, with most between ₹1,000 and ₹1,300. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
