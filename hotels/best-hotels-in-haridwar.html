Where to stay in Haridwar

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

  • Most stays are in Railway Station (178), Bhupatwala (151), Upper Road & Mayapur (132).
  • 635 have a star rating, including 33 five-star and 153 four-star.
  • 148 (12%) allow pets and 167 (13%) are on or facing the Ganga.

Coming for the Kumbh 2027?

Haridwar fills up months ahead of the Kumbh. Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at, expect road closures around the big snan days, and book early. For a calmer base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver.

Best Hotels in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many stays are there in Haridwar?

There are 1,269 stays in Haridwar on this page, spread across 15 areas. The biggest clusters are Railway Station (178), Bhupatwala (151), Upper Road & Mayapur (132).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for stays?

For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road. Railway Station is handy for trains, a short ride from the ghats; Bhupatwala is an ashram-lined stretch on the way towards Rishikesh, calmer than the centre; Upper Road & Mayapur is the bazaar strip behind the ghats, walkable to Har Ki Pauri.

How much does a stay in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay at about ₹2,600 a night, with most between ₹1,600 and ₹3,900. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly stays in Haridwar?

Yes. 148 of the 1,269 stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
