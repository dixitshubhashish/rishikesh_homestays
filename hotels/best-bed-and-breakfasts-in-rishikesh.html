Best Bed & Breakfasts in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many bed & breakfasts are there in Rishikesh?

There are 35 bed & breakfasts in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 8 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (19), Laxman Jhula (6), Muni Ki Reti (3).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for bed & breakfasts?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Muni Ki Reti is between Ram Jhula and the town, with ashrams, ghats and easy road access. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a bed & breakfast in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical bed & breakfast at about ₹2,500 a night, with most between ₹2,000 and ₹3,700. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly bed & breakfasts in Rishikesh?

Yes. 5 of the 35 bed & breakfasts here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
