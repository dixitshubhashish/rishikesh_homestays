Best Stays with a Pool in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many stays with a pool are there in Rishikesh?

There are 137 stays with a pool in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 13 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (74), Neelkanth Road (16), Shivpuri & rafting belt (5).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for stays with a pool?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Neelkanth Road is the hilly road towards Neelkanth Mahadev, with resorts and camps; you will need a vehicle; Shivpuri & rafting belt is upstream on the Badrinath road where most rafting starts, with riverside camps.

How much does a stay with a pool in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical stay with a pool at about ₹5,700 a night, with most between ₹3,600 and ₹12,800. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
