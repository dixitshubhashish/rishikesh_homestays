Advaitam Ganga & Hill View: A Homely Rishikesh Stay for Families & Groups

Bring your whole group to a spacious home with 3 bedrooms, 7 beds, 3 bathrooms, a comfortable living room, and peaceful Ganga and hill views. Step out for the evening aarti, order in when you like, and settle into Rishikesh at your own pace.

A private home near Ganges that feels easy to settle into!

Advaitam Ganga & Hill View is one of the better large-group options for a Rishikesh stay: an entire 3BHK apartment where your family or friends can share meals, relax after a day out, and still have room to spread out. It is in the quieter Nirmal Bagh area, close enough to the river for a daily ritual but away from constant market noise.

Wake up to fresh Himalayan air and hill views, walk 50m to the ghat for the evening Ganga Aarti, and return to the comfort of a homely base. It works especially well for family holidays, yoga groups, spiritual trips, friend groups, workations, and longer staycations.

Riverside rituals, city convenience

The Ganga is part of the daily rhythm here, not just a sightseeing stop. The nearby ghat is around 50m away, with an evening aarti within an easy walk.
