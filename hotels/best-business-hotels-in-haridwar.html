Choosing business hotels in Haridwar

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

  • Most business hotels are in Rishikesh Road & Motichur (10), Shantikunj & Saptrishi (9), Bhupatwala (9).
  • The typical listed starting price is about ₹2,600 a night; the middle half of business hotels start between ₹1,800 and ₹4,400 (based on 71 listings with a price).
  • 73 have a star rating, including 6 five-star and 19 four-star.
  • 15 (20%) allow pets and 13 (17%) are on or facing the Ganga.
  • Of the 44 with at least five guest reviews, 6 score 9/10 or higher.

Coming for the Kumbh 2027?

Haridwar fills up months ahead of the Kumbh. Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at, expect road closures around the big snan days, and book early. For a calmer base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver.

Best Business Hotels in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many business hotels are there in Haridwar?

There are 76 business hotels in Haridwar on this page, spread across 14 areas. The biggest clusters are Rishikesh Road & Motichur (10), Shantikunj & Saptrishi (9), Bhupatwala (9).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for business hotels?

It depends on the trip. Rishikesh Road & Motichur is on the road towards Rishikesh, near Motichur and Chandi Devi; Shantikunj & Saptrishi is the riverside stretch towards Rishikesh around Shantikunj and Saptrishi; Bhupatwala is an ashram-lined stretch on the way towards Rishikesh, calmer than the centre. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a business hotel in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical business hotel at about ₹2,600 a night, with most between ₹1,800 and ₹4,400. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly business hotels in Haridwar?

Yes. 15 of the 76 business hotels here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
