Choosing resorts in Rishikesh

Resorts are usually outside the walkable centre, so budget for taxis or a car. In return you get space, pools and views. Check whether the price includes meals, and how far the resort is from the ghats if you plan to see the evening aarti.

Which area of Rishikesh is best for resorts?

It depends on the trip. Neelkanth Road is the hilly road towards Neelkanth Mahadev, with resorts and camps; you will need a vehicle; Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Shivpuri & rafting belt is upstream on the Badrinath road where most rafting starts, with riverside camps. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a resort in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical resort at about ₹5,700 a night, with most between ₹3,500 and ₹9,600. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
