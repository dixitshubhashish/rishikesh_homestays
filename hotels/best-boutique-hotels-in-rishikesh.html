Best Boutique Hotels in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many boutique hotels are there in Rishikesh?

There are 15 boutique hotels in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 6 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (8), Muni Ki Reti (2), Shivpuri & rafting belt (1).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for boutique hotels?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Muni Ki Reti is between Ram Jhula and the town, with ashrams, ghats and easy road access; Shivpuri & rafting belt is upstream on the Badrinath road where most rafting starts, with riverside camps.

How much does a boutique hotel in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical boutique hotel at about ₹100 a night, with most between ₹0 and ₹100. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly boutique hotels in Rishikesh?

Yes. 6 of the 15 boutique hotels here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest boutique hotel on this page?

La Casa Bonita – Boutique Homestay in Tapovan has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹11 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.

Which boutique hotels here have the best guest reviews?

The best-reviewed are Lamrin Boutique Cottages (9.2/10), Atreya Boutique Cottage near Tapovan (9.2/10), Trippme Rishikesh – Boutique Hostel, Tapovan (8.7/10), counting only stays with at least ten reviews.
