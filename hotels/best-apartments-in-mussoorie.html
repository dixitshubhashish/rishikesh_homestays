Choosing apartments in Mussoorie

Mussoorie is a long ridge town, so where you sleep decides how you spend the day. The Mall Road between Library Chowk and Kulri is the lively centre, with shops, cafes and the evening walk; Library Chowk and Charleville, at the western end, are older and a little calmer; Camel's Back Road and Gun Hill are for slow evening strolls; Landour and Char Dukan, to the east, are quieter, with old cottages and bakeries; Barlowganj and Jharipani sit on the slopes below; and the Kempty Road runs out towards Kempty Falls, with many of the resorts and views. On busy weekends and holidays the Mall Road area gets crowded, so ask your stay how far a car can go and how much of the way is on foot.

Best Apartments in Mussoorie: questions travellers ask

How many apartments are there in Mussoorie?

There are 60 apartments in Mussoorie on this page, spread across 9 areas. The biggest clusters are Mall Road & Kulri (20), Landour & Char Dukan (11), Library Chowk & Charleville (10).

Which area of Mussoorie is best for apartments?

For the Mall Road, cafes and evening walks, stay around Library Chowk or Kulri; for quiet, try Landour or Camel's Back Road; for views and the falls, the Kempty Road. Mall Road & Kulri is the lively centre, with the Mall Road, Kulri bazaar, cafes and the evening walk; Landour & Char Dukan is the quieter, older side to the east, with old cottages, bakeries and walks; Library Chowk & Charleville is the western end of the Mall Road, a little calmer than Kulri, with the old library and shops.

How much does a apartment in Mussoorie cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical apartment at about ₹7,600 a night, with most between ₹3,700 and ₹10,000. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
