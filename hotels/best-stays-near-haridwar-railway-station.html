Staying near Haridwar Railway Station: good to know

The station is about 2 km from Har Ki Pauri; e-rickshaws run the route all day.

Arriving late or leaving early? Confirm the check-in time, ask the stay to hold your room for a late arrival, and book a cab the evening before an early train.
