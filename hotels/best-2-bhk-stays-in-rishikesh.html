Choosing 2 BHK stays in Rishikesh

A 2 BHK is the sweet spot for a family of four or two couples travelling together: two real bedrooms, a shared living room and usually a kitchen, often for about the price of two hotel rooms. Before you book, check that both bedrooms have proper beds rather than a sofa-cum-bed, how many bathrooms there are, whether there is a lift and parking for your car, and what the power backup runs, as short cuts still happen.

Best 2 BHK Stays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 49 two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 11 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (22), Veerbhadra Temple (7), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (6).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats.

How much does a 2 BHK stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical 2 BHK stay at about ₹100 a night, with most between ₹0 and ₹100. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
