Choosing 2 BHK stays in Rishikesh

A 2 BHK is the sweet spot for a family of four or two couples travelling together: two real bedrooms, a shared living room and usually a kitchen, often for about the price of two hotel rooms. Before you book, check that both bedrooms have proper beds rather than a sofa-cum-bed, how many bathrooms there are, whether there is a lift and parking for your car, and what the power backup runs, as short cuts still happen.

Best 2 BHK Stays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 60 two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 13 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (26), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (8), Veerbhadra Temple (7).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a 2 BHK stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical 2 BHK stay at about ₹5,300 a night, with most between ₹4,200 and ₹9,900. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 21 of the 60 two-bedroom (2 BHK) stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
