Best Ashram Stays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many ashram stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 14 ashram stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 8 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (5), Veerbhadra Temple (2), Laxman Jhula (2).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for ashram stays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a ashram stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical ashram stay at about ₹2,400 a night, with most between ₹1,100 and ₹2,500. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly ashram stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 3 of the 14 ashram stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
