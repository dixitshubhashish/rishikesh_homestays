Haridwar Hotels for Family: what to know

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

Haridwar Hotels for Family: questions travellers ask

How many hotels for family are there in Haridwar?

There are 100 hotels for family in Haridwar on this page, spread across 12 areas. The biggest clusters are Railway Station (16), Har Ki Pauri (11), Upper Road & Mayapur (9).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for hotels for family?

For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road. Railway Station is handy for trains, a short ride from the ghats; Har Ki Pauri is the main ghat and pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti; Upper Road & Mayapur is the bazaar strip behind the ghats, walkable to Har Ki Pauri.

How much does a hotel in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical hotel at about ₹3,600 a night, with most between ₹2,400 and ₹5,900. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
