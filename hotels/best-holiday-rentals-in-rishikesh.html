Best Holiday Rentals in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many holiday rentals are there in Rishikesh?

There are 99 holiday rentals in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 15 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (18), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (11), Triveni Ghat (7).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for holiday rentals?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Triveni Ghat is the town centre, with the big evening aarti, markets and the bus stand nearby.

How much does a holiday rental in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical holiday rental at about ₹3,300 a night, with most between ₹2,300 and ₹4,200. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly holiday rentals in Rishikesh?

Yes. 6 of the 99 holiday rentals here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.

What is the cheapest holiday rental on this page?

Yogi Home Stay Gulab Nagar Tapovan Inter Collage in Tapovan has the lowest listed starting price here, from ₹957 a night. Prices change with dates, so check the current rate before you book.

Which holiday rentals here have the best guest reviews?
