How many holiday rentals are there in Rishikesh?

There are 207 holiday rentals in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 16 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (34), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (19), AIIMS Rishikesh (14).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for holiday rentals?

For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; AIIMS Rishikesh is near the AIIMS hospital on the Haridwar side, practical for hospital visits.

How much does a holiday rental in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical holiday rental at about ₹3,300 a night, with most between ₹2,300 and ₹4,200. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.
