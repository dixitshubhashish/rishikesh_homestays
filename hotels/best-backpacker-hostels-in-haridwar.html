Choosing backpacker hostels in Haridwar

Haridwar is built around its ghats. Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road are the walkable pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti and the busiest at festivals; Kankhal and Bhupatwala are calmer, ashram-lined neighbourhoods; Shantikunj and Saptrishi sit along the river towards Rishikesh; Jwalapur, Ranipur and the Delhi Road are practical bases with easier parking. For big snan days, stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at.

Best Backpacker Hostels in Haridwar: questions travellers ask

How many backpacker hostels are there in Haridwar?

There are 6 backpacker hostels in Haridwar on this page, spread across 4 areas. The biggest clusters are Railway Station (3), Har Ki Pauri (1), Shantikunj & Saptrishi (1).

Where should I stay in Haridwar for the Kumbh 2027?

Stay within walking distance of the ghat you plan to bathe at: Har Ki Pauri and the Upper Road for the main snans, or Kankhal, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj for calmer bases a short walk or ride away. Roads close to vehicles around the big bathing days, so a walkable stay matters more than a fancy one. Book as early as you can; for a quieter base, Rishikesh is about 25 km upriver. Our Haridwar Kumbh 2027 guide covers the reported dates and planning.

Which area of Haridwar is best for backpacker hostels?

It depends on the trip. Railway Station is handy for trains, a short ride from the ghats; Har Ki Pauri is the main ghat and pilgrim centre, closest to the evening Ganga aarti; Shantikunj & Saptrishi is the riverside stretch towards Rishikesh around Shantikunj and Saptrishi. For the evening aarti and the main snans, stay near Har Ki Pauri or the Upper Road; for a calmer base, Kankhal, Bhupatwala or Shantikunj; for driving in and out, the Delhi Road or Rishikesh Road.

How much does a backpacker hostel in Haridwar cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical backpacker hostel at about ₹1,400 a night, with most between ₹700 and ₹1,400. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly backpacker hostels in Haridwar?

Yes. 1 of the 6 backpacker hostels here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
