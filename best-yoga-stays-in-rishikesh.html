Best Yoga Stays in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many yoga stays are there in Rishikesh?

There are 226 yoga stays in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 16 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (115), Laxman Jhula (31), Veerbhadra Temple (9).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for yoga stays?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Laxman Jhula is around the Laxman Jhula bridge, close to temples, cafes and the river; Veerbhadra Temple is a residential area south of the centre, quieter but a ride from the ghats. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a yoga stay in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical yoga stay at about ₹3,700 a night, with most between ₹2,100 and ₹6,400. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly yoga stays in Rishikesh?

Yes. 85 of the 226 yoga stays here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
