Best Holiday Rentals in Rishikesh: questions travellers ask

How many holiday rentals are there in Rishikesh?

There are 14 holiday rentals in Rishikesh on this page, spread across 8 areas. The biggest clusters are Tapovan (4), Nirmal Bagh near Ganges (4), Ram Jhula (1).

Which area of Rishikesh is best for holiday rentals?

It depends on the trip. Tapovan is the busy cafe, yoga-school and backpacker hub above Laxman Jhula; walkable and lively; Nirmal Bagh near Ganges is a quieter residential pocket close to the river and ghats; Ram Jhula is around the Ram Jhula bridge, between Swarg Ashram and Muni Ki Reti. For the evening aarti and ashrams, stay near Swarg Ashram, Muni Ki Reti or Triveni Ghat; for rafting, stay towards Shivpuri.

How much does a holiday rental in Rishikesh cost per night?

Listed starting prices put a typical holiday rental at about ₹3,300 a night, with most between ₹2,300 and ₹4,200. Prices rise on weekends, long weekends and festivals, and fall in the monsoon.

Are there pet-friendly holiday rentals in Rishikesh?

Yes. 7 of the 14 holiday rentals here list pets as allowed. Confirm the pet policy and any extra fee with the property before booking.
